About

Computer science across research and education.

My work spans software engineering, Computer Science education and research. I am particularly interested in formal verification of computational geometry and computer-graphics algorithms, including collision detection and graphics architectures designed with native machine-learning and computer-vision capabilities.

Across projects, I prioritise explicit architecture, reproducible experiments, careful testing and documentation. In education, I aim to connect mathematical foundations with accessible programming exercises and independent problem solving.
